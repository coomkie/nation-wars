# Nation Wars Live — Unique Unit Concepts

This document introduces a set of unusual/creative unit ideas selected for the game, described at a concept level (visual identity, mechanic, and design rationale).

| Unit | Description | Mechanic | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bomb Carrier** | A soldier carrying a large bomb strapped to their back instead of a weapon. Doesn't fight directly while alive — the danger is in its death. | melee (weak in life) + aoe + stun | Flips the usual "death = loss" logic: when this unit is killed, the bomb detonates, dealing area damage and stunning enemies caught in the blast radius. Turns dying into a tactical payoff rather than a pure loss. |
| **Plague Rat** | A single oversized, diseased rat — sickly green fur, dripping with toxic ooze. Not a swarm, just one large creature. | melee + aoe (poison damage-over-time) | Instead of a hard-hitting single strike, it inflicts a lingering poison effect that deals small continuous damage to anything nearby, making it hard to counter with single big hits and rewarding sustained area presence. |
| **Bell Ringer** | A robed figure carrying a massive bronze bell instead of any weapon. Doesn't advance toward the enemy base. | aoe + stun (stationary support) | Stands near its own base and periodically rings the bell, releasing a sound wave that stuns all enemies within range. Acts like a slow-moving defensive tower rather than a conventional attacker — a purely area-control support unit. |
| **Anchor Dragger** | A sailor dragging a massive iron anchor instead of wielding a weapon. Moves very slowly. | melee + knockback + stun | Each swing of the anchor deals damage, knocks the target back, and has a chance to stun — trading extreme movement speed for a powerful all-in-one crowd-control hit. A clear "slow but devastating" archetype. |
| **Snail Cavalry** | A rider mounted on a giant snail instead of a horse — the polar opposite of a fast cavalry unit. | melee + knockback | Very slow but extremely tanky, and leaves a trail of slime behind it that temporarily slows any enemy unit crossing it — turning its own slowness into an environmental effect rather than a pure weakness. |
| **Rainmaker Shaman** | A shaman who summons a localized rain cloud instead of attacking directly. | aoe debuff (stationary support) | Deals no damage — instead, it creates a rain zone around itself that slows the movement speed of any enemy unit passing through. The first purely environmental/debuff support unit, opening up a "control the battlefield" playstyle distinct from direct combat. |
| **Molting Cicada Warrior** | A warrior wearing cracked, shell-like armor resembling a cicada's exoskeleton, with translucent wing-like cloak fragments on its back. | melee (revives once with reduced stats) | When its HP first reaches 0, instead of dying outright, it cover its with shell and gain hp, after 5 second, if the the hp still remain, it sheds its shell and reforms into a lighter, faster but weaker version of itself with a small HP pool — this "molt" can only happen once per unit. Creates a unit that feels genuinely hard to finish off without being unkillable, adding tension to close fights instead of a clean, predictable kill. |

## Support Unit Spawn Rule

Support-type units — units that do not fight directly and instead provide area buffs/debuffs or crowd control from a stationary position (e.g. **Bell Ringer**, **Rainmaker Shaman**, and other units following this pattern) — are limited to **a maximum of 1 active unit per nation at a time**.

- A player/viewer cannot stack multiple support units of the same type on the battlefield simultaneously.
- A new support unit of that type can only be spawned once the previously active one has been destroyed.
- This rule prevents stacking of powerful area-control effects (mass stun, mass slow) that could otherwise make matches feel unfair or one-sided, while still allowing the support role to remain impactful and worth fighting to protect or destroy.
